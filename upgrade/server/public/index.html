Timing & track limits

Cross the line to start timing. Use the kerbs. Brief excursions get a recovery window; sustained cuts delete the lap. Races allow three warnings before a five-second penalty. Resetting carries a penalty. Time Trial runs for three timed laps; Practice stays open. Negative delta means you are ahead of your best lap.

Session rules

Qualifying sets the grid. A quick race starts from the back. Full qualifying runs 18 · 15 · 13 minutes, with six drivers eliminated in Q1 and Q2. Finish a timed lap after the flag.

P · RB calls the crew. T · LB selects the next tyre. Pit entry, limiter and release are assisted. Tyre life scales with race distance.
