Timing & track limits

Cross the line to start timing. Use the kerbs. Brief excursions get a recovery window; sustained cuts delete the lap. Races allow three warnings before a five-second penalty. Resetting carries a penalty. Time Trial runs for three timed laps; Practice stays open. Negative delta means you are ahead of your best lap.
