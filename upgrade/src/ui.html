DRIVER BRIEFING

Driving guide

  • W / ↑ throttle · S / ↓ / Space brake · A / D / ← / → steer.
  • C cycles chase, nose, wide chase and aerial cameras. R resets the car. Esc pauses. F toggles fullscreen.
  • Controllers: left stick to steer, RT / R2 to accelerate, LT / L2 to brake.
  • Racing line: green to accelerate, amber to lift, red to brake. Follow the arrows through the bends. Press L or use the pause menu to change Full / Corners / Off. Suggested speed is a guide, not automatic braking.
  • Assists are on. Steer smoothly and brake before tight corners.
  • Cross the start line to begin timing. Leaving the kerbs or resetting invalidates that lap.
  • Complete three timed laps in Time Trial. Practice stays open until you end the session.
  • A negative delta means you are ahead of your best valid lap this session. Your personal best saves on this browser.
